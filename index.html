A note from the founder

From “my life” to “our life.”

I started American Honeymooner because I want couples to feel excited about their honeymoon—not just relieved to have booked it. This is the beginning of your married life. There should be room for romance, little rituals, and a story you didn’t plan.

This is your permission to order the wine, stay one more night, take the balcony room, and tell everyone you’re newlyweds. Not because you expect anything—because being married is worth celebrating.

The American Honeymooner guide

Make it feel like a honeymoon.

01

Choose the feeling first

Start with how you want the trip to feel—not a list of places you think you’re supposed to go.

02

Let people celebrate you

Add it to the hotel note, mention it at dinner, and create little moments where the world can toast the newlyweds.

03

Leave room for one beautiful yes

The boat at sunset. The dessert wine. The extra night. A honeymoon needs room for surprise.
